## A. Cell filled (legal / D. Custody): legal wrapper with no natural-person control point

- New §agent-custody synthesis on
  [approaches-legal](../pages/approaches-legal.md); new approaches.md row under
  D. Custody, maturity `theory`.
- **Verdict: no legal form exists today through which an agent holds assets
  with no natural person anywhere in the control chain.** Four independent
  termination layers, each falsifiable:
  1. *Entity formation* — Wyoming DAO Supplement **W.S. 17-31-114(a)(v)**
     dissolves a DAO "no longer … under the control of at least one (1)
     natural person" (text extracted this run from the Secretary of State's own
     DAO Supplement PDF; the SOS *Statement of Intent to Dissolve* form carries
     that event as a checkbox with a signature line). Wyoming **DUNA**
     (SF0050) needs ≥100 members. RMI DAO LLC is the only production statute
     that removes the human from *management* (§708 smart-contract management)
     but keeps four terminators: mandatory registered agent (§705(2) names
     MIDAO Directory Services in the statute), Representative Agent with
     signature/phone (2024 Regs §4(1)), beneficial-owner report with passport
     (§712(2)), and background checks before certification (§9(2)(e)–(f)).
     Delaware's AIC bill is still a draft (1 member; not sent to the GA, which
     returns Jan 2027; Comer/Oversight opened an investigation 26 Oct 2026).
  2. *AML/CDD* — **FinCEN 31 CFR 1010.230(d)** names individuals in both
     prongs; the 11 Aug 2026 final rule exempts US companies but foreign
     reporting companies still report foreign individuals; **EU AMLR Art.
     22(2)** resolves to senior managing officials; **MiCA Art. 59(1)(a)**
     needs a Union-resident director. FATF is *not* a natural-person rule —
     INR.10 contemplates no controlling natural person — but Glossary fn 96
     ("the ultimate beneficial owner is always one or more natural persons")
     plus the senior-managing-official fallback put a natural person at the end
     of every CDD chain; the July 2026 DeFi report confirms truly
     decentralised arrangements fall outside the Standards entirely.
  3. *Formation/tax* — **IRS Form SS-4**: "the responsible party must be an
     individual (that is, a natural person), not an entity."
  4. *Contract/attribution* — UETA's "electronic agent" attributes to the
     principal; **9th Cir., Amazon v. Perplexity (4 Aug 2026)**: "it is a tool,
     not a person for statutory purposes."
- **Estonia's agent code is the inverse of personhood**: bound to a *physical
  person*, act deemed that person's own; ministry memo says the identifier
  "ei ole õigussubjekt" (not a legal subject) / "on tehniline vahend"
  (technical means); narrowed in consultation to private individuals, one
  agent per person, companies excluded; government-approved 12 Nov 2026, still
  before the Riigikogu.
- **Real-world wrappers all keep a human**: ai16z (SDNY: DAO = "an
  unincorporated association"; Eliza Labs, Inc. held the treasury; foundation
  wound down after settlement), Truth Terminal (Truth Collective non-profit
  "until AIs are allowed to own their own property"), Virtuals (Cayman
  foundation company, GLEIF LEI active, independent professional director),
  Bittensor (Opentensor Foundation, Canadian corp #13026531, directors Steeves
  and Shaabana), Ocean (Cayman Ocean Expeditions, sole director Bruce Pon),
  Olas (no entity at all — treasury on-chain, site "operated by Crypto in
  Motion"), AgentFi (no wrapper; docs: a wallet is "not proof that an AI legally
  owns money"). Uniswap's DUNI (Wyoming DUNA, adopted 9 Sep 2025) is the most
  production-grade wrapper and still appoints the Uniswap Foundation as
  Ministerial Agent plus a contracted Administrator.
- Prototypes all keep a human deliberately: ETHGlobal Lisbon 2026 winner Novi
  Corpus ships a Wyoming DAO LLC with a "legally required human controller, the
  'guardian', because US law … simply does not allow a company with zero humans
  behind it."
- open-questions: P1b legal cell **answered and struck**; 4 new questions
