## A. Cell filled (distributed-mind / F. Continuity): lineage/speciation tracking

- New §lineage synthesis on
  [approaches-distributed-mind](../pages/approaches-distributed-mind.md);
  approaches.md row "Lineage/speciation tracking" marked filled (maturity
  stays `research`).
- Verdict: the tracking machinery is mature and cost-measured **inside
  simulation** — Avida (v2.14, still canonical but OpenSSF scorecard says
  0 commits/90 days, i.e. dormant), Phylotrack (arXiv:2405.09389) and the
  distributed-specific guide arXiv:2405.10183 ship the tooling; Cerebras WSE
  digital-evolution runs (arXiv:2608.23955) give the cost ceiling: 226.1M
  agents / 5M generations at ~8,750 gen/s, exhaustive parent-child tracking
  impractical at ~quadrillion replication events, so lineage is recovered
  by **sample-and-reconstruct** (~8B genomes harvested in 10 min → 1.1B
  kept, ~0.0001%, tolerating 5% bit-flip corruption) — lineage at population
  scale is bandwidth-bound, not compute-bound.
- **In the wild, no shipped system records an LLM agent's lineage.**
  Spore.fun (15 agents, 1 running), Truth Terminal (model change,
  operator-mediated), superorganism (dormant 5 Jul) are all
  operator/narrative-managed; *History Is Not Memory* shows state copies
  leave zero residue, so forks are invisible by construction. The four
  "what would have to be true" conditions are on the page (signed
  mutation events; record survives the operator; forks compete for
  reputation/revenue so pedigree has selective value; sampling fits the
  bandwidth budget). Falsifiable trigger: first live agent whose
  fork/spawn events are provably signed by its own key and third-party
  readable.
- open-questions: two P3s added (signed-fork-record watch; Avida dormancy
  watch).
