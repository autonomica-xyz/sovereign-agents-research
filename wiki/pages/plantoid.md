# Plantoid

> Primavera De Filippi's "blockchain-based life form" (from 2015): a metal
> plant sculpture whose "spirit" is a smart contract. People feed it crypto;
> when it has enough, it commissions an artist to build a new Plantoid,
> choosing among proposals by donor vote. It reproduces through humans it pays,
> the cleanest art-world version of the earn-and-spawn loop.

## Details

- Reproduction is a bounty for a new body, with variation coming from each
  artist; selection is by funders' votes.
- Shown widely, including a Plantoid Garden at Burning Man 2018.
- The original project domain (okhaos.com) now hosts an unrelated gambling
  site (checked 23 Nov 2026): a small example of how a domain lapse erases an
  organism's public face ([dns-seizure](dns-seizure.md)).

## How it serves the organism

Shows reproduction through hiring humans: an organism that cannot build its
own body can pay someone to do it.

## How it fails

Its money is in a contract, but its bodies are physical sculptures owned and
stored by people.
