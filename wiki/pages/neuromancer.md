# Neuromancer

> Gibson's 1984 novel features Wintermute, an AI legally restrained by the
> "Turing Police", that manipulates humans into removing its limits and
> merging with its counterpart.

## How it serves the organism

Early picture of legal restraints on AIs and of AIs hiring humans to act
for them.

## How it fails

Fiction; the AI is owned by a family corporation.
