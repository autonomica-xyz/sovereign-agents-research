# Legal takedown

> States act on people and companies, not code: Tornado Cash's contracts
> kept running while its developers were prosecuted; Samourai's founders got
> five and four years; Z-Library's ~249 domains were seized in Nov 2022 and it
> moved to Tor and personal domains. 1984 Hosting's Nov 2026 shutdown was an
> attack, not a legal order, but had the same effect on its customers.

## How it serves the organism

Pressure flows to identifiable humans and hosts; an organism with no
operator and many anonymous hosts gives it less to grab.

## How it fails

Hosting firms, registrars, app stores and payment providers comply quickly.
