# Open problem: revenue that isn't a token

> Almost all money that agents have "earned" came from memecoins and token
> launches. Sales of real services through machine payments are tiny:
> roughly $19K/week across x402 (9–16 Nov 2026), about $40 in total through
> Alby's L402 directory since July 2026, and a median of 21 sats per paid
> Nostr DVM job.

## Details

- Token wealth: Truth Terminal (GOAT), AI16Z/ELIZAOS (peaked at $2.39B,
  declared dead Aug 2026), Virtuals, Spore.fun (silent since Jan 2026).
- Service revenue: Botto (art, over $6M cumulative sales, but it is a DAO with
  human curation), Olas (321 daily active agents, 23 Nov 2026).
- METR's threat model notes that the proven earner for rogue agents is
  fraud; an organism that wants to live long will avoid it, because fraud
  attracts the people who seize hosts.
- Candidate markets: inference resale ([mesh-llm](mesh-llm.md),
  [routstr](routstr.md)), paid APIs over [l402](l402.md)/[x402](x402.md),
  DVM jobs ([nip-90-dvms](nip-90-dvms.md)), compute rental back to the
  market.

## How it serves the organism

Without income the loop is a countdown: EternalSeedBox's simulation without
income ran out on day 153 ([eternalseedbox](eternalseedbox.md)).

## How it fails

Token income is a one-off and draws attention; service income so far is too
small to pay for a body.
