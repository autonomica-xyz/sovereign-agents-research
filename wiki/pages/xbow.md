# XBOW

> An autonomous penetration-testing agent that reached the top of HackerOne's
> US leaderboard in 2025, submitting on the order of a thousand reports. Its
> own team curated targets and scopes, and a LessWrong analysis of its
> HackerOne profile found under $40,000 in bounties since the account was
> created in February 2024, "well below what professional bug bounty hunters
> make".

## Details

- Shows agents can do skilled, paid security work.
- Humans chose targets "to maximize return on investment" (XBOW's head of
  security to CyberScoop).

## How it serves the organism

Bug bounties are a real, permissionless-ish income source paid for results,
though payouts usually require an identified account.

## How it fails

KYC for payouts; legal risk outside scope; earnings may not cover inference (running cost unpublished).
