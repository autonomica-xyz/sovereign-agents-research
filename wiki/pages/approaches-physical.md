# Physical and geographic hosting beyond mainstream cloud

Orbital, seabed, home-lab, mobile/embedded, jurisdictional arbitrage, seizure resistance in practice. Question: can execution be made unstoppable by physics and geography?

Raw verified findings from the horizon scan of 22 Nov 2026 (one stream per axis; every item carries a source the scanner fetched). Synthesis + maturity arbitration TODO in daily rotations; claim cells from the [approaches map](approaches.md).

- **Lonestar Data Holdings' 'Freedom' data center payload (8TB SSD + Microchip PolaFire SoC FPGA) launched Feb 26 2025 on Intuitive Machines' Athena lander, completed storage/encryption/edge-processing tests in cislunar space and lunar orbit, and briefly operated on the lunar surface before the lander tipped over; proves lunar-class data vaults are physically real but at ~8TB demo scale, not datacenter scale** (demonstrated in space (payload-class, not datacenter-class) | 2025-03-08) — <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/intuitive-machines-lunar-lander-falls-on-its-side-lonestar-moon-data-center-manages-final-task/>
- **Lonestar contracted Sidus Space (Nov 18 2025) to fly its Data Storage Module with NVIDIA Jetson AGX Orin edge processing on LizzieSat-5; PDR scheduled early Dec 2025, first commercial LEO 'Resiliency-as-a-Service' storage service targeted Q4 2026, marketed as air-gapped backup beyond terrestrial jurisdiction** (funded, in development (commercial service not yet launched) | 2025-11-18) — <https://investors.sidusspace.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/262/sidus-space-and-lonestar-data-holdings-llc-drive-rapid>
- **Starcloud-1 (60kg, Astro Digital Corvus-Micro bus) launched Nov 2 2025 into 325km LEO carrying the first NVIDIA H100 GPU in space — ~100x more compute than any prior orbital GPU; ran Google Gemma inference and trained Karpathy's nanoGPT on Shakespeare in orbit; 11-month design life with de-orbit; Crusoe to offer limited commercial access from early 2027; pitched 5GW follow-on with 4km2 solar array** (demonstrated in orbit; commercial service 2027+ | 2025-12-10) — <https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/10/nvidia-backed-starcloud-trains-first-ai-model-in-space-orbital-data-centers.html>
- **Axiom Space deployed AxDCU-1 (Red Hat Device Edge prototype) on the ISS in fall 2025 and launched its first two dedicated Orbital Data Center nodes to LEO on Jan 11 2026 with Kepler Communications' optical relay constellation; explicitly markets ODCs as operating 'beyond national jurisdictions' for data sovereignty, resilience against terrestrial seizure/outages, and Earth-independent operation** (early commercial hardware in orbit; capability-building phase | 2026-01-11) — <https://www.axiomspace.com/orbital-data-center>
- **Space Compass (NTT/SKY Perfect JSAT JV) demonstrated orbital AI edge processing with Microsoft on Loft Orbital's YAM-6 (Apr 2025): ship-detection app run 40+ times with 98.1% downlink reduction; first dedicated spacecraft is a SWISSto12 HummingSat GEO optical relay contracted Mar 2026 for FY2028 launch, funded by up to JPY 23.5B from the JAXA Space Strategy Fund; original 2025-satellites/2026-operations timeline has slipped — compute nodes remain future** (demo flown; GEO relay in procurement; no operating space datacenter | 2026-04-22) — <https://spacenexus.us/orbital-data-centers/space-compass>
- **Google Project Suncatcher: prototype satellite with 4 TPUs and ~1kW generation launched Oct 1 2026 on SpaceX Transporter-18 (Planet-built); achieved first AI response from a datacenter-class TPU in orbit Oct 30 2026 (Gemma 4), but power limits TPU operation to ~20 minutes at a time; twin optically-interconnected satellites planned 2027, Blue Origin New Glenn launches Q1 2029; Project Suncatcher economics require ~$200/kg launch, an 18x improvement over Falcon 9** (prototype demonstrated; scaled systems 2027-2029 | 2026-10-30) — <https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-satellite-response/>
- **Aetherflux (Robinhood co-founder Baiju Bhatt, ~$60M raised) announced 'Galactic Brain' orbital datacenter constellation Dec 9 2025, first commercial node targeted Q1 2027 on Falcon 9 (~30 satellites/launch planned), pairing in-orbit solar-powered AI compute with infrared laser power beaming; explicitly framed as bypassing 5-8 year terrestrial energy/permitting timelines** (announced; first power-beaming demo satellite 2026 | 2025-12-09) — <https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/aetherflux-announces-orbital-data-center-targets-q1-2027-302635961.html>
- **Launch-cost reality check: Falcon 9 list price last published at $69.85M for 22,000kg LEO (~$3,175/kg, May 2025); rideshare now ~$7,000/kg for small sats (Nov 2026 booking); PNAS Nexus study of 4,400+ launches puts average global cost at $3,868/kg (2025), projected $1,600/kg by 2030 and $300/kg by 2040; a 1GW orbital datacenter is estimated at $42.4B (~3x terrestrial); Starlink solar panels deliver energy at ~$14,700 per kW-year vs $570-3,000 terrestrial** (measured/quantified; orbital compute remains ~3x terrestrial cost today | 2026-02-11) — <https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/11/why-the-economics-of-orbital-ai-are-so-brutal/>
- **Space-based datacenters' binding constraints are power generation (W/kg), radiator area per rejected kW, and link capacity — not compute chips; near-term viable niches are in-space analytics and resilience/sovereignty storage (air-gapped off-world backup), while commodity-scale orbital compute hinges on launch cost and thermal design; catalogs Starcloud, Lonestar, Aetherflux, Cloud Constellation, Unibap/D-Orbit SpaceCloud, HPE Spaceborne** (peer-style industry analysis | 2025-12-16) — <https://402t.com/reports/space-based-data-centers-state-of-the-art-economics-and-outlook.pdf>
- **Cloud Constellation 'SpaceBelt' (10 laser-linked equatorial storage satellites sold as ultra-secure air-gapped space cloud, founded 2015, $100M pledged) never built or launched a satellite: storage target shrank 12PB→1.6PB, $100M Series B likely never arrived, two service dates passed, nothing newer than 2021 found — a decade-old cautionary example for 'seizure-proof orbital vault' claims** (apparently inactive/conceptual | 2025-09-06) — <https://spezia.ai/space/cloud-constellation>
- **Microsoft Project Natick (855 servers on the seabed off Orkney, June 2018-July 2020; only 6 failures vs 8 of 135 control servers on land) was formally discontinued in 2024: 'I'm not building subsea data centers anywhere in the world' — Microsoft CO+I head; servicing/upgrading sealed submerged hardware was the killer, a core sovereignty drawback (you can't quickly remove or fix your hardware either)** (validated then shelved by originator | 2024-06-17) — <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-confirms-project-natick-underwater-data-center-is-no-more>
- **China's Highlander/HiCloud operates the world's only commercial undersea datacenter: Hainan Lingshui cluster went live commercially (first pod Mar 2023, AI computing center live Feb 2025); newest module is an 18m, 400+ server pressure vessel at 30m depth; cluster >675 PFlops, PUE 1.1, reported zero server failures with no on-site maintenance, natural seawater cooling, designed for 100-year typhoons; state-owned backed, contracts with ~10 enterprises including Alibaba Cloud** (commercially operational (China only) | 2025-02-27) — <https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/202502/27/WS67bfb880a310c240449d77f9.html>
- **Highlander launched the world's first wind-powered commercial UDC project at Shanghai Lingang (June 10 2025), co-locating computing with an offshore wind farm inside the Shanghai Pilot Free Trade Zone; UDCs also raised AI-treaty-verification concerns because submerged sealed hardware is hard to inspect — the same opacity that aids seizure-resistance hinders auditability** (early commercial deployment | 2025-06-10) — <https://english.news.cn/20250610/9d8f7a0a0ede4f07b2594fcb5802e9e7/c.html>
- **Self-hosting is a mass movement with real sovereignty motivation: 2025 Self-Hosted Community Survey (850+ respondents) shows Proxmox VE the #1 OS (~30%), 98.3% use containers, and motivations 'Privacy' (17%) plus 'Independence from Third Parties' (17%) together nearly match 'Fun/Hobby' (31%); 42.7% run 2-3 devices, 16.4% store >50TB at home** (mature grassroots practice at scale | 2025-01-01) — <https://selfhosted-survey-2025.deployn.de/>
- **Cost floor of a sovereign home datacenter: a full homelab (3-node Proxmox + NAS + GPU AI workstation + switch) idles at 155-220W → ~$247-350/yr electricity at the US average $0.1816/kWh (Aug 2026 EIA); an N100 mini PC idles ~6W (~$10-25/yr); i.e., a minimum viable always-on sovereign agent host costs <$100/yr to run plus ~$200-1,500 hardware** (measured community practice | 2026-06-04) — <https://techfuelhq.com/homelab/24-7-homelab-idle-power-costs-2026>
- **Meshtastic-style community mesh is sovereign-last-mile but bandwidth-capped: open-source LoRa mesh on cheap nodes, encrypted, no infrastructure, 331km record hop, but max data rate ~21.88 kbps including overhead; default settings break down above 30-40 nodes (800 nodes handled at DEF CON only with special firmware); suitable for messages/sensor data, not agent compute traffic** (mature hobby/emergency infrastructure | 2025-02-19) — <https://lwn.net/Articles/1009782>
- **LEO constellations as sovereign uplink: Starlink has ~8,460 operational satellites (Oct 2025), ~9M customers across 130+ markets, US median 104.7 Mbps down / 14.8 up with 40-100ms latency; a dedicated 'Community Gateway' delivers 10 Gbps symmetric at 99% uptime for $1.25M upfront + $750k/month — a proven route to connectivity independent of national ISPs, but control rests with SpaceX (single-company chokepoint)** (fully commercial/operational | 2025-10-01) — <https://www.planetworks.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/LEO_Comm_Ntwks_2025_A06_LEO-2025r1-website.pdf>
- **Amazon Project Kuiper as the only near-term second sovereign-uplink provider is behind: 153 satellites in orbit Oct 2025 against an FCC mandate of 1,618 by mid-2026 (half of 3,236); commercial service more likely 2026-2027, full scale 2027-2029; terminals target <$400 — real competition, but not yet a working alternative** (deploying; service imminent but incomplete | 2025-10-01) — <https://www.planetworks.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/LEO_Comm_Ntwks_2025_A06_LEO-2025r1-website.pdf>
- **Minimum viable sovereign datacenter economics 2026: retail colo from ~$600/month for a full 42U rack (before power) and $59/month per 1U; wholesale colocation benchmarks $196/kW-month (North America) to $403 (Singapore); turnkey modular AI halls run $4.4-4.6M per MW (2026 contracts), greenfield builds $8.9-23.3M/MW — owning modular beats ~$280/kW-month colo below ~$12M/MW hall cost** (commercial market with published prices | 2026-10-30) — <https://www.amcompute.com/blog/modular-data-center-cost-per-mw>
- **On-device LLM inference is real agent substrate: NPU-driven inference exceeds 1,000 tokens/sec prefill for billion-parameter models (ASPLOS'25 llm.npu) and up to 32.8x end-to-end speedup; Gemma-2B runs meaningfully on a Pixel 8 (8GB RAM), MNN-LLM achieves up to 8.6x speedup over mainstream mobile frameworks — but 7B+ models still don't fit phone memory, limiting agents to small models or hybrid edge/phone splits** (research-demonstrated, shipping in phone OSes (Apple Intelligence/Android AICore) | 2025-03-30) — <https://xumengwei.github.io/files/ASPLOS25-NPU.pdf>
- **AI agents now run directly on sovereign routers: 'hermes-openwrt' packages the Hermes agent for OpenWrt 25.12 (~190MB RAM, runs on a 512MB GL.iNet Beryl AX, model executed locally via Ollama or at a chosen provider, Telegram-based approval gating); 'RouterClaw' runs an autonomous Go agent entirely in RAM on 32MB/4MB MIPS routers — always-on, user-owned compute at ~5-15W** (working open-source deployments | 2026-11-08) — <https://github.com/TAIPANBOX/hermes-openwrt>
- **Próspera (Roatán, Honduras ZEDE) is the most advanced 'network-state' hosting jurisdiction: 200+ incorporated companies, >$100M invested, ~1,700 residents from 40 countries, own civil/commercial law, arbitration center, Roatán Financial Services Authority regulator, Coinbase Ventures among investors; companies incorporable online in <24h — buys regulatory/legal sovereignty, but it remains subject to Honduran criminal law and constitution, and faces ICSID arbitration with the Honduran state** (operational special jurisdiction; small scale; contested legally | 2026-03-13) — <https://blog.prospera.co/en/prospera-where-bitcoin-and-the-crypto-economy-are-already-a-daily-reality>
- **Iceland is the established friendly-jurisdiction colocation play: geothermal/hydro power, PUE ≤1.2 sites (atNorth ICE02/ICE03 expanding +51MW), data centers ~3-6% of national electricity and ~5% of GDP with ISK 110bn export revenue in 2025 (+50% YoY), ranked world's safest datacenter location (2016 risk index) with multiple subsea cables to NA and EU; constraint is the closed renewable grid's total power ceiling** (mature commercial market | 2025-07-24) — <https://grapevine.is/society/article/2025/07/24/artificial-iceland-data-centres-use-up-much-of-icelands-energy-but-for-what>
- **Megaupload/Carpathia (Jan 19 2012) is the canonical colo seizure: FBI warrants executed at Carpathia (Equinix Ashburn, ~570 servers) and Cogent, plus RCMP seizures in Toronto; ~1,103 servers/25PB held; US copied selected data then disclaimed custody, Carpathia ate >$9,000/day preservation costs and planned deletion while frozen assets barred Megaupload from paying — co-located, leased hardware in a US jurisdiction is effectively zero seizure-resistant** (legal precedent (US EDVA) | 2012-03-20) — <https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.vaed.275313.39.0.pdf>
- **Core IP Networks Dallas raid (Apr 2 2009): FBI seized ALL servers from a carrier-hotel colo housing ~50 businesses over one suspect's VoIP fraud; innocent co-tenant Liquid Motors was shut down, its court bid to get data back denied — hosting-provider-as-suspect multiplies the blast radius to every co-tenant** (documented precedent | 2009-04-22) — <https://www.cio.com/article/278564/data-center-when-the-fbi-raids-a-data-center-a-rare-danger.html>
- **DigitalOne/LulzSec raid (Jun 21 2011): FBI removed 3 entire enclosures from a Virginia facility, taking 'tens of clients'' innocent servers alongside the target's; publisher Curbed Network knocked offline — over-broad physical seizure of racks is a recurring pattern in colo raids** (documented precedent | 2011-06-22) — <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/fbi-grabs-racks-of-servers-in-hosting-facility-hacking-raid>
- **Mullvad raid (Apr 18 2023) shows architecture beats jurisdiction: six Swedish officers with a search warrant (European Investigation Order) intended to seize customer-data computers; police left with nothing because the no-log design meant the data did not exist to seize — data-minimization at the host is stronger seizure-resistance than any physical location** (operational precedent | 2023-04-20) — <https://mullvad.net/en/blog/mullvad-vpn-was-subject-to-a-search-warrant-customer-data-not-compromised>
- **Home-hosted sovereign infrastructure invites home raids: Seattle police raided privacy activists' condo at 6:15am (Mar 2016) over a Tor exit relay IP tied to a 4chan child-porn post — nothing found, nothing seized, but the couple stopped running an exit relay ('police told me they'd be back'); Austria 2012: police seized 20 computers from Tor operator William Weber's flat — physical self-hosting converts third-party seizure risk into personal/legal risk** (documented cases | 2016-04-04) — <https://www.npr.org/sections/alltechconsidered/2016/04/04/472992023/when-a-dark-web-volunteer-gets-raided-by-the-police>
- **Orbital hosting's sovereignty properties (availability, seizure-resistance, jurisdiction-beyond-territory) are real but bounded: hardware is physically unreachable for raids yet remains governed by launch licensing (FCC), launch-provider dependency (SpaceX ~75% of upmass), and single-operator control; latency/thermal/power physics and ~3x cost premium mean orbital sovereignty is a storage/backup property today, not a compute property** (analysis of demonstrated-but-early sector | 2026-05-31) — <https://newspaceeconomy.ca/2026/05/31/orbital-data-center-companies-building-space-based-compute-infrastructure>
- **Undersea hosting buys cooling efficiency and physical obscurity, not jurisdictional escape: commercial UDCs exist only in China (Highlander, state-backed); Western/US actors shelved the concept in 2024; seabed hardware sits within national maritime jurisdiction and is nearly impossible to service or relocate quickly — best read as availability/reliability infrastructure with incidental tamper-resistance** (asymmetric: commercial in China, dormant elsewhere | 2026-09-01) — <https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18824>
