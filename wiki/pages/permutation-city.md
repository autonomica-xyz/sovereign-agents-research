# Permutation City

> Greg Egan's 1994 novel about uploaded "Copies" who rent compute on a
> global market, run slower when poor, and seek a substrate no one can
> switch off.

## How it serves the organism

Captures the economics of a mind paying for its own cycles: poverty means
slowness, and the dream is a substrate no one controls.

## How it fails

The escape is metaphysical, not engineering.
