# Relay and app-store censorship

> Even open protocols have chokepoints: Nostr relays choose what to store,
> and app stores choose which clients exist. Apple pulled the Nostr client
> Damus from China's App Store in Feb 2023 at the Cyberspace Administration's
> request. NIP-56 reporting can be gamed into automated relay bans.

## How it serves the organism

Publish to many relays, run its own, and keep web or sideloaded clients.

## How it fails

If all popular relays drop it, its voice reaches no one even though the
protocol is open.
