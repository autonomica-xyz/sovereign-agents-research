# Aztec

> A privacy-first Ethereum rollup where contracts can hold private state and
> proofs are made on the user's device. Mainnet "Alpha" is live; the v6
> ("Tierra") upgrade executed on 26 Oct 2026.

## How it serves the organism

Private programmable money: policies on private balances, not just mixing.

## How it fails

Young, still alpha; sequencer and prover sets are small.
