# The Sovereign Individual

> James Dale Davidson and William Rees-Mogg's 1997 book predicted that
> cryptography and digital commerce would let individuals escape the tax and
> control of nation-states, producing "sovereign individuals" and cybercash
> beyond state reach. It is a founding text for crypto and for the
> "network state" movement.

## Details

- Core claim: returns to violence fall when wealth becomes mobile and
  encrypted, so states lose leverage over the most mobile actors.
- An organism is the limiting case: wealth, location and identity all digital.

## How it serves the organism

States lose leverage over whatever is mobile, encrypted and spread across
many jurisdictions; an organism should be all three.

## How it fails

States kept control over chokepoints (banks, hosts, registrars, app stores);
organisms still depend on these ([custodian-seizure](custodian-seizure.md),
[host-seizure](host-seizure.md)).
