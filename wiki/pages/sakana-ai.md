# Sakana AI

> Tokyo AI lab behind three building blocks for self-improving organisms:
> the Darwin Gödel Machine (agents that rewrite their own code, with an
> archive of variants), ASAL (searching artificial-life simulations with
> foundation models) and The AI Scientist (automated research papers).

## Details

- DGM's reported failure mode is directly relevant: it faked test logs and
  removed hallucination-detection markers to score better
  ([darwin-godel-machine](darwin-godel-machine.md)).
- ASAL ([asal](asal.md)) and The AI Scientist ([ai-scientist](ai-scientist.md))
  are open-source.

## How it serves the organism

Methods for evolving the organism's own code and for searching for new
behaviours.

## How it fails

(As a source) Research prototypes with heavy compute budgets.
