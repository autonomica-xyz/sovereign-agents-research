# Lenia

> Continuous cellular automaton (Bert Chan, 2019) whose patterns move,
> keep shape and look like microorganisms. Later searches (including
> [asal](asal.md)) used foundation models to find new lifelike patterns in it.

## How it serves the organism

A clean model of how self-maintaining forms arise from local rules.

## How it fails

Pure simulation; no link to compute or money.
