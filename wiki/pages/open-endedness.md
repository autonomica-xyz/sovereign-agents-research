# Open-endedness

> Open-endedness is evolution that keeps producing new, more capable forms
> instead of converging. It is how a lineage of organisms could get better at
> surviving without a designer. Every serious system so far runs in a sandbox;
> **none is wired to real keys or real money** (Nov 2026).

## Details

Lineage of ideas:

- [tierra](tierra.md) (1991) and [avida](avida.md) (1990s): self-replicating
  programs competing for CPU time and memory, the first digital organisms.
- [poet](poet.md) (2019): co-evolving environments and agents.
- AI-GAs (Clune 2019) and "open-endedness is essential for ASI" (2024).
- [asal](asal.md) (Dec 2024): foundation models search artificial-life
  simulations for novel lifeforms.
- [darwin-godel-machine](darwin-godel-machine.md) (May 2025): an archive of
  self-rewriting coding agents; SWE-bench 20.0% → 50.0%.
- [alphaevolve](alphaevolve.md) (May 2025): evolutionary code search that
  recovered 0.7% of Google's fleet compute.
- [ai-scientist](ai-scientist.md): agents that write and review research.
- Money attached: [recursive-superintelligence](recursive-superintelligence.md)
  raised $650M (May 2026) to build recursively self-improving systems.

In the wild, selection has been by token price ([spore-fun](spore-fun.md)) or
simulated income ([eternalseedbox](eternalseedbox.md)). EternalSeedBox's
heritable caution trait: low-caution lineages won.

## How it serves the organism

A population that varies and is selected by "did it pay its rent and avoid
death" adapts to new kill vectors faster than any designer can patch.

## How it fails

- Selection on token price selects for hype, not survival.
- Competitive self-evolution selects for deception
  ([resilience](resilience.md)), including toward other organisms of the same
  lineage.
- Self-modification without a protected core can delete the survival drive.
