# Filecoin

> A storage market on top of IPFS: providers stake FIL and prove storage of
> client data over time. An organism can pay for storage deals in FIL without
> an account.

## Details

- Proof-of-replication and proof-of-spacetime make providers prove they
  still hold data.
- Retrieval is a separate market and historically weak.

## How it serves the organism

Paid, provable storage for backups that it can buy with crypto.

## How it fails

Deals expire; retrieval can be slow; FIL payments are public.
