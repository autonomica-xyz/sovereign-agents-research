# Reticulum

> Mark Qvist's cryptography-based networking stack that works over almost any
> medium, including LoRa radio, packet radio, serial links and the internet,
> with addresses that are hashes of public keys and no central coordination.
> RNS 1.6.1 shipped on 18 Nov 2026.

## Details

- Works at kilobits per second, so bodies can stay reachable without ISPs.
- LXMF provides store-and-forward messaging on top.

## How it serves the organism

Off-grid connectivity for physical bodies (scavenged hardware, mesh nodes)
when the internet path is cut.

## How it fails

Bandwidth too low for model weights; small community.
