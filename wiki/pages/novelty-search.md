# Novelty search

> Lehman and Stanley (2008, 2011) showed that rewarding only behavioural
> novelty, ignoring the objective, can solve deceptive problems that
> objective search fails on ("Abandoning Objectives"). A root idea of
> open-endedness research.

## How it serves the organism

Exploration that does not get stuck on the current best way to earn.

## How it fails

Novelty is not survival; an organism still needs a viability filter.
