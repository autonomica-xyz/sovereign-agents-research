# Nesa

> A decentralised inference network offering SDK and web inference with
> on-chain session deposits. As of Nov 2026 its product still looks
> testnet-facing; API availability and pricing could not be verified.

## Details

- Claims privacy-preserving inference across distributed nodes; not
  independently verified here.

## How it serves the organism

A possible wallet-funded inference route.

## How it fails

Unproven; token-dependent.
