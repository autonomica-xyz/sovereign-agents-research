# AI personhood bans

> Idaho (Code §5-346) and Utah (Code §63G-32-102) have enacted laws denying
> legal personhood to AI; Ohio's HB 469, which would declare AI non-sentient,
> was still in committee (Oct 2026). Several other states considered bans in
> 2026.

## Details

- The bans target recognition (marriage, property, standing), not entity
  wrappers run by software.
- Federal pressure on state AI laws (Executive Order 14365, Dec 2025)
  complicates the picture ([approaches-legal](approaches-legal.md)).

## How it serves the organism

Tells it where not to incorporate and which courts will refuse it standing.

## How it fails

Pushes organisms to wrappers with human fronts, which are seizure points.
