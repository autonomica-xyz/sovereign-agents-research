# Arm CCA

> Arm's Confidential Compute Architecture (Armv9) runs VMs in "Realms"
> managed by a Realm Management Monitor, isolated from the hypervisor by
> granule protection. As of late 2026 most development uses emulation
> (FVP, QEMU); Fujitsu's MONAKA is the clearest production candidate, with
> shipments planned for 2027.

## How it serves the organism

A third CPU vendor family for TEE diversity, and a path to phones and edge
devices.

## How it fails

Not deployable at scale yet.
