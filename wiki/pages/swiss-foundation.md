# Swiss foundation

> A Swiss Stiftung has no owners: its assets belong to the foundation and are
> bound to its stated purpose. The Ethereum Foundation (Stiftung Ethereum, Zug,
> incorporated 14 July 2014) made it the classic crypto wrapper. Foundations
> with international activity are supervised by the Federal Supervisory
> Authority for Foundations (ESA).

## Details

- Purpose is fixed at founding and hard to change, a kind of legal
  constitution ([conway-automaton](conway-automaton.md) uses a code
  constitution for the same idea).
- A board (natural persons) runs it; the supervisory authority checks that
  assets serve the purpose.

## How it serves the organism

The closest legal analogue to an ownerless entity with an immutable purpose:
"keep this organism running" could be the purpose.

## How it fails

The board are people who can be compelled; the supervisor can intervene;
Swiss banks require identification of controllers.
