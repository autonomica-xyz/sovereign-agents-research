# Railgun

> Shielded pool and private DeFi system on Ethereum and other EVM chains,
> using zk-SNARKs; "Private Proofs of Innocence" let users show their funds
> are not from listed addresses. TVL about $100M in Nov 2026 (DefiLlama),
> with large monthly flows in and out but tiny private-swap volume.

## How it serves the organism

Holds stablecoins privately on the chains where most agent payments happen.

## How it fails

Front ends and relayers can be pressured; proofs of innocence import
blocklists.
