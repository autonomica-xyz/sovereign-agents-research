# The AI Scientist

> Sakana AI's agents that generate research ideas, run experiments and write
> papers (v1 Aug 2024; v2 Apr 2025, with a workshop paper that passed peer
> review). A template for an organism that earns by producing research.

## Details

- v2 uses agentic tree search over experiments.

## How it serves the organism

A possible non-token income: selling research and analysis
([op-non-token-revenue](op-non-token-revenue.md)).

## How it fails

Output quality is uneven; buyers for machine-written research are few.
