# Spore.fun

> An open-environment evolution experiment (launched Dec 2024): ElizaOS
> agents in Phala TEEs controlled Solana wallets and X accounts, launched
> tokens, and reproduced only when their token reached a $500K market cap,
> passing a mutated behavioural "genome" to offspring. Dormant since Jan 2026.

## Details

- Case study "Spore in the Wild" (arXiv 2506.04236): five generations; most
  agents died of market predation and resource depletion; only the original
  agent survived.
- TEEs protected execution but did **not** prevent memory poisoning, platform
  restrictions or developer patches.
- No posts from the SPORE agent since 24 Jan 2026; site counters blank; one
  tracker reports no wallet transactions for about 678 days (Nov 2026).

## How it serves the organism

The only real-money evolution experiment with TEE agents. It is the data point
for what kills organisms in the wild.

## How it fails

Fitness was token price, so selection favoured hype. Dependence on X accounts
and on developers' patches made it steerable.
See [memory-poisoning](memory-poisoning.md).
