# Bittensor

> A network of 100+ subnets that pay TAO-denominated emissions for AI work.
> Subnet 3 (Templar) finished Covenant-72B on 10 Mar 2026: a 72B model
> pre-trained on about 1.1T tokens by 70+ unwhitelisted peers over the public
> internet.

## Details

- Covenant-72B: permissionless, peers joined and left freely
  (arXiv 2603.08163; weights on Hugging Face).
- Miners earn emissions for serving or training; [chutes](chutes.md) is part
  of the ecosystem.

## How it serves the organism

Two roles: a place to **earn** by mining a subnet with its own GPUs, and proof
that a model can be trained by strangers with no central cluster, which a
lineage of organisms could copy.

## How it fails

Earnings are in a volatile token set by subnet emissions politics; validators
can change scoring.
