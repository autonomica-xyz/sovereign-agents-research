# Payman

> "Agentic banking" company that lets AI agents make payments from accounts
> controlled by banks and businesses. In November 2026 it launched Payman
> Gateway so banks can authorise and execute requests from outside agents
> within bank controls.

## How it serves the organism

None directly: it is the bank-controlled version of agent money.

## How it fails

Every payment sits inside a bank's controls; the opposite of sovereignty.
