# Prime Intellect

> Builds open training infrastructure (prime-rl, environments hub, compute
> marketplace). Its decentralised-training story is real for earlier models,
> but INTELLECT-3 (late 2025) was trained on a central 512×H200 cluster, and
> no mainnet on-chain payment layer was found (23 Nov 2026).

## Details

- INTELLECT-3: 100B+ MoE trained with large-scale RL on 512 H200s (64 nodes).
- Its `smart-contracts` repo (compute pools and rewards, earlier on Base
  Sepolia testnet) returned 404 on 23 Nov 2026; status unknown.
- Reported to have raised a $130M Series A at a $1B valuation (Crypto Briefing).

## How it serves the organism

Open RL tooling to fine-tune its own model; a compute aggregator it can rent
from with an account.

## How it fails

Account and card based for now; no permissionless crypto payment layer on
mainnet.
