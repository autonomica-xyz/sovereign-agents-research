# Privy

> Embedded-wallet provider (keys split with secure enclaves, created at login
> with email or social accounts) used by many consumer crypto apps and agent
> frameworks. Stripe announced it would acquire Privy on 11 Jun 2025; Privy keeps operating as a separate product.

## How it serves the organism

Quick wallet onboarding for agents launched by apps.

## How it fails

The app developer and Privy sit in the trust path; now part of Stripe, a
KYC'd payments company.
