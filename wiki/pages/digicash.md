# DigiCash and Chaumian ecash

> David Chaum's blind signatures (1982) let a bank sign a coin without seeing
> it, so spending cannot be linked to withdrawal. His company DigiCash ran
> trials in the 1990s and went bankrupt in 1998. The same scheme is now
> [cashu](cashu.md) and [fedimint](fedimint.md), the most private money an
> organism can hold.

## Details

- Payer privacy, not payee privacy: the mint sees who redeems.
- Central mint: a single company or federation can refuse service
  ([custodian-seizure](custodian-seizure.md)).

## How it serves the organism

Lineage of the ecash rails it uses for small, private payments.

## How it fails

DigiCash died for lack of adoption by banks; Cashu mints today can die for
the same reasons, or be shut down.
