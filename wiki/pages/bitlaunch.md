# BitLaunch

> Hourly VPS paid in BTC, ETH or LTC with email-only signup and a public API
> to create, manage and pay for servers. It resells DigitalOcean, Vultr and
> Linode capacity alongside its own.

## Details

- API-driven, so a program can buy and destroy servers.

## How it serves the organism

A SporeStack-like path still open in Nov 2026 ([sporestack](sporestack.md)).

## How it fails

Reseller: the upstream cloud's abuse desk can end servers; no TEE; email
account is a recovery and identity point.
