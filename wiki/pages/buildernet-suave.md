# BuilderNet and SUAVE

> Flashbots' SUAVE was a proposed chain for private, decentralised block
> building; in practice the work became BuilderNet, a TDX-based block
> builder run jointly by Flashbots, Beaverbuild and Nethermind with
> reproducible images and attestation. Flashbots said its systems were
> patched before Intel's Aug 2026 TDX disclosures.

## How it serves the organism

Proof that attested TDX images can run high-value, adversarial workloads in
production.

## How it fails

Few operators; Intel-only.
