# Cayman foundation company

> Under the Cayman Islands' Foundation Companies Law 2017, a foundation
> company can have **no members** if its constitution allows it, as long as it
> keeps at least one supervisor. It can hold assets and contract with third
> parties while its by-laws route decisions to token holders or code. It became
> the default wrapper for large DAO treasuries.

## Details

- Supervisor: a non-member with the right to attend and vote at meetings and
  oversee directors.
- Directors execute; the constitution can bind them to on-chain votes.

## How it serves the organism

Memberless entity with offshore courts and no tax on the entity; it can own
the organism's legal-world assets (domains, contracts) with no owner to seize.

## How it fails

Directors and the supervisor are people; Cayman follows international
sanctions and beneficial-ownership regimes.
