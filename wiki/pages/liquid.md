# Liquid Network

> A Bitcoin sidechain run by a federation: 15 functionaries (one per member
> chosen to run one), blocks and the peg wallet need 11 of 15 signatures from
> keys in HSMs. Confidential Transactions hide amounts and asset types.
> Hosts L-BTC and tokenised assets such as USDT.

## How it serves the organism

Fast settlement with hidden amounts; good for holding a stablecoin
float privately from amount-watchers.

## How it fails

The federation members are known companies; 11 of 15 can freeze or censor.
