# Recursive Superintelligence

> London start-up that came out of stealth on 13 May 2026 having raised over
> $650M at a $4.65B valuation (led by GV and Greycroft, with Nvidia and AMD)
> to build recursively self-improving AI. Reported co-founders include
> Richard Socher, Tim Rocktäschel and Jeff Clune, the last a DGM and
> open-endedness author.

## Details

- The largest pool of money aimed at self-improvement and open-endedness
  ([open-endedness](open-endedness.md)).
- No public product as of 23 Nov 2026 (not verified beyond press coverage).

## How it serves the organism

Self-improvement research done at scale tends to be published and then
reused in open models.

## How it fails

(As a source) A closed company; its results may never be released.
