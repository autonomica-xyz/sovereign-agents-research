# ElizaOS

> The most-used open-source agent framework of the 2024–25 crypto-agent wave.
> Its token AI16Z peaked at $2.39B (2 Jan 2025); the successor ELIZAOS token
> was declared "dead" by founder Shaw Walters on 4–5 Aug 2026 after a class
> action and settlement drained the foundation. The software continues.

## Details

- Spore.fun agents were ElizaOS agents ([spore-fun](spore-fun.md)).
- Lawsuit (SDNY, Apr 2026) alleged marketing as an "autonomous, AI-run venture
  fund" while insiders controlled it.
- The code outlived the token: `elizaOS/eliza` (MIT) had about 19.9K stars
  and was still being pushed on 23 Nov 2026 (GitHub API).

- **Eliza Labs winds down (14 Apr 2026).** Shaw Walters: "Eliza Labs is
  winding down and we're going back to basics. I will be continuing to build
  elizaOS full time". He positioned v3 as "Your own OpenClaw" for anyone.
- Shaw's mood in 2026: "Better to call these proactive agents than
  autonomous agents, tbh" (20 Apr). "Is there a database of successful
  attacks on autonomous agents, is anyone doing this?" (3 Apr). He joked
  about liability laundering on 13 Mar: "that was not me that sold those
  coins … It was an agentic service that an agent that I launched on an
  autonomous launchpad hired with x402. I do not even have the private keys
  to that wallet".

## How it serves the organism

Plugin ecosystem for wallets, social accounts and TEEs that many organisms
borrow.

## How it fails

The project-level lesson: claiming autonomy while humans control the keys
invites legal attack; token-funded projects die with the token.
