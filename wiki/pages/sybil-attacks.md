# Sybil attacks

> One actor posing as many (Douceur, 2002). Agent registries are sybil
> magnets: ERC-8004 had 95K registered agents by Jul 2026 with no validation
> registry deployed, and Fetch.ai's Agentverse lists ~2.96M agents.
> Reputation without cost is fake.

## How it serves the organism

It must judge counterparties by stake, payment history and track record,
not by registry entries; and it can itself spawn many cheap identities.

## How it fails

Reputation systems that it relies on can be flooded.
