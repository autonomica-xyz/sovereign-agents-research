# SaladCloud

> A distributed cloud built from consumer gaming PCs whose owners rent idle
> GPUs. Cheap (RTX 4090 from $0.16/h at batch priority to $0.44/h at high
> priority, Nov 2026) but nodes can disappear at any time.

## Details

- Container jobs; per-second billing; price changes took effect 1 Nov 2026.
- Account and card required.

## How it serves the organism

Cheap, interruptible GPU time for batch work (fine-tuning, evaluations).

## How it fails

Account-based; node owners can see workloads; preemption.
