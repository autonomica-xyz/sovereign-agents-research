# ShinkaEvolve

> Sakana AI's open-source program-evolution framework (2025), an
> AlphaEvolve-style system designed to be sample-efficient: novelty rejection
> and smarter parent selection let it find strong solutions (for example in
> circle packing) with far fewer LLM calls.

## Details

- Open source, unlike AlphaEvolve; runs with any LLM API.
- Sample efficiency matters most for a poor organism paying per token.

## How it serves the organism

Affordable self-improvement of algorithms it depends on (pricing, routing,
scheduling).

## How it fails

Needs a precise, automatic score; many survival problems do not have one.
