# Marshall Islands DAO LLC

> The Republic of the Marshall Islands' Decentralized Autonomous Organization
> Act 2022 (P.L. 2022-50) lets a DAO register as a domestic LLC that is
> member-managed or algorithmically managed, with token-based membership and
> actions recorded on-chain. A 2023 amendment (P.L. 2023-83) added series
> DAO LLCs, securities carve-outs and beneficial-owner rules. MIDAO is the sole
> registered agent.

## Details

- First sovereign country (not a sub-national state) with a DAO LLC form.
- Non-profit and for-profit variants; registration within 30 days after the
  2023 amendment.
- Beneficial owners above a threshold must be disclosed to the registered
  agent.

## How it serves the organism

An offshore legal body that accepts on-chain governance, outside US and EU
courts' direct reach.

## How it fails

Single registered agent (MIDAO) is a chokepoint; beneficial-owner
reporting; a small state can be pressured by larger ones.
