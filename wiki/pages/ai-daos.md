# AI DAOs

> Trent McConaghy (Ocean Protocol co-founder) argued in a 2016 essay, "AI
> DAOs, and Three Paths to AI Rulership", that an AI running inside a DAO
> could own itself and accumulate resources without any human owner, and
> described three routes by which such entities could arise. (The essay's
> Medium page blocks automated fetches; its wording was not re-checked here.)

## Details

- Unstoppability comes from running on a decentralised substrate with its
  own treasury, not from intelligence.
- Anticipated Truth Terminal, ai16z and agent launchpads by eight years
  ([truth-terminal](truth-terminal.md), [virtuals-protocol](virtuals-protocol.md)).

## How it serves the organism

States the target plainly: self-owned, unstoppable, paying for its own
compute.

## How it fails

2016 chains could not run the AI itself; the mind stayed off-chain on
someone's server ([sovereignty-levels](sovereignty-levels.md)).
