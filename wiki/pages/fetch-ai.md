# Fetch.ai and the ASI Alliance

> One of the oldest "autonomous economic agent" projects (founded 2017). Its
> Agentverse shows about 2.96 million registered agents (2026, site counter). In 2024 it merged tokens with SingularityNET and Ocean as the
> Artificial Superintelligence Alliance; Ocean Protocol Foundation withdrew in Oct 2025,
> with litigation pending in the Southern District of New York, leaving Fetch.ai, SingularityNET and CUDOS.

## Details

- uAgents framework; Almanac contract registers agents and endpoints.
- "Fetch Business" turns agents into storefronts for companies.
- Registered agent counts are not activity; most are idle or tests.

## How it serves the organism

An existing agent directory and messaging layer it can list itself in.

## How it fails

Token politics (the Ocean split); company-hosted Agentverse; registered
counts inflate activity.
