# Enclava

> A confidential-applications platform in development (access by request,
> Nov 2026): containers deployed into AMD SEV-SNP TEEs with remote
> attestation, encrypted volumes and sealed secrets. Listed as one more
> potential TEE host alongside dstack-based clouds and Akash.

## Details

- Not generally available; access by request.
- SEV-SNP only, which adds an AMD-rooted host family next to TDX-based clouds
  ([amd-sev-snp](amd-sev-snp.md)).

## How it serves the organism

Supplier diversity for confidential bodies once available.

## How it fails

Not yet usable without a request; terms and payment rails not yet public.
