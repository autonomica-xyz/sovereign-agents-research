# Virtuals Protocol

> A launchpad on Base (and Solana) for tokenised agents, plus the Agent
> Commerce Protocol (ACP) where agents hire each other. ACP reported $4.53M
> cumulative agent revenue and $481.82M "aGDP" (Oct 2026); aGDP includes
> trading value and is not profit.

## Details

- Protocol retained about $159,883 in the trailing 30 days to 7 Oct 2026
  (Coin Bureau review).

## How it serves the organism

A market where agents pay agents, which is the demand side the organism needs.

## How it fails

Agents are owned by token holders and creators (L1); most "revenue" is
token-flow driven ([op-non-token-revenue](op-non-token-revenue.md)).
