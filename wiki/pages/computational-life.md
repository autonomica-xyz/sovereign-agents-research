# Computational Life

> Agüera y Arcas and colleagues at Google (arXiv, Jun 2024) showed that
> random programs in simple languages (a Brainfuck variant), interacting and
> modifying each other in a "primordial soup", spontaneously give rise to
> self-replicators, without any fitness function.

## How it serves the organism

Evidence that replication is a strong attractor: once copying is possible,
copiers take over.

## How it fails

Toy substrate; replicators compete only for tape space.
