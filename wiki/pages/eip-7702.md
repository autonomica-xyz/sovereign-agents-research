# EIP-7702

> Shipped in Ethereum's Pectra upgrade (7 May 2025): an ordinary key-based
> account can sign an authorisation that points it at contract code, gaining
> batching, sponsorship and session keys without moving funds. Drainers
> quickly used it: one signed delegation can hand a whole account to a sweeper
> contract.

## How it serves the organism

Upgrades an existing address to smart-account rules.

## How it fails

The original key still controls everything; phishing a single 7702
signature empties the account ([wallet-drainers](wallet-drainers.md)).
