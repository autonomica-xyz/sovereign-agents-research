# Wyoming DAO LLC and DUNA

> Wyoming was the first US state to recognise DAOs: the DAO LLC (in force
> 1 July 2021) may be "algorithmically managed" by smart contracts, and the
> Decentralized Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (DUNA, SF0050, 2024)
> gives non-profit DAOs with at least 100 members a legal form governed by
> consensus algorithms. Amendments followed in 2026.

## Details

- DAO LLC: limited liability, profit distribution allowed, smart contract
  can be the operating agreement; must name a registered agent in Wyoming
  and file annually.
- DUNA: non-profit purpose; no profit distributions to members; 100-member
  minimum; meant for protocols and treasuries.
- Neither makes the code a person; the entity is the person, the code is its
  manager.

## How it serves the organism

A US legal body whose management can be a smart contract the organism
controls: it can hold a bank account or sign a hosting contract.

## How it fails

US jurisdiction, a registered agent who can be served, and beneficial-owner
rules. If the organism is the only real "member", the wrapper points
straight at whoever formed it.
