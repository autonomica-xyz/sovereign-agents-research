# 1984 Hosting

> An Icelandic host that accepts BTC and XMR and needs only an email. It has
> no VPS API, so a program cannot buy servers without driving a web UI. On
> 21 Nov 2026 it suspended all services after an intrusion that began on
> 12 Nov; as of 23 Nov the main service, dashboard and mail were still down
> (nameservers up).

## Details

- Iceland is a common "friendly jurisdiction" choice
  ([approaches-physical](approaches-physical.md)).
- Payment: card, PayPal, Bitcoin and Monero; crypto is not offered to users
  who appear to be in Iceland (tax rules). Prepaid 1 or 3 years for hosting.
- **Nov 2026 incident:** CEO Mörður Áslaugarson told mbl.is (23 Nov) the
  attack began on 12 Nov, escalated about six days later, destroyed seven
  pro-Palestine websites, and looked like a well-funded state-level APT; he
  said customer data was not touched and the company would report it to
  police. Everything was shut down to clean up. Separately, the founder of
  Wagyu blamed 1984 for retained data after the XMR1 token collapsed >99%;
  1984 rejects that.
- For an organism this is a supplier outage of 9+ days with no warning: any
  body hosted there was offline regardless of its own health
  ([supplier-shutdown](supplier-shutdown.md)).

## How it serves the organism

Jurisdiction diversity for one of its bodies.

## How it fails

No API, no TEE; manual ordering makes it a poor fit for autonomous
reproduction.
