# Accelerando

> Charles Stross's 2005 novel (released free under a Creative Commons
> licence) follows a family through a singularity in which companies become
> autonomous programs, "Economics 2.0" squeezes out humans, and a robot cat,
> Aineko, turns out to be a superintelligence.

## How it serves the organism

Imagines self-incorporating software and agent-run markets decades before
DAOs.

## How it fails

Fiction; skips the hosting and payment chokepoints that dominate reality.
