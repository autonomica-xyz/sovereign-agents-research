# Yggdrasil

> An experimental end-to-end encrypted IPv6 overlay mesh where each node's
> address is derived from its public key and routing needs no central
> authority.

## Details

- Peers over the internet or local links; addresses survive moves between
  networks.
- Not anonymous: peers see each other's IPs.

## How it serves the organism

Stable key-derived IPv6 addresses for its bodies across hosts.

## How it fails

No anonymity; still labelled alpha.
