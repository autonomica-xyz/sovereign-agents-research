# Coinbase AgentKit and CDP Agentic Wallet

> Coinbase's developer platform gives agents wallets that can hold, trade and
> pay for x402 services via CLI or MCP, with spend limits and guardrails. Keys
> are managed by Coinbase's infrastructure (TEE-backed server wallets), not by
> the agent.

## Details

- AgentKit: framework-agnostic toolkit for on-chain actions.
- Coinbase co-founded the x402 Foundation ([x402](x402.md)).

## How it serves the organism

Easiest way to get a working wallet and x402 client.

## How it fails

Custodial in practice: Coinbase can freeze the wallet or the developer
account ([custodian-seizure](custodian-seizure.md)).
