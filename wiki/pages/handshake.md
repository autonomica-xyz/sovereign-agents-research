# Handshake

> A proof-of-work blockchain (mainnet 2020) that acts as an alternative DNS
> root: top-level names are auctioned and owned by keys. Still running in
> Nov 2026, but with thin infrastructure: an exchange (DxPool) announced it
> would close HNS withdrawals on 30 Nov 2026.

## Details

- Owning a TLD on Handshake means no ICANN registry can seize it; resolution
  needs HNS-aware resolvers or gateways.
- Most browsers do not resolve Handshake names natively.

## How it serves the organism

A top-level name that belongs to a key.

## How it fails

Few resolvers, few users, thin liquidity: a name nobody's browser can reach.
