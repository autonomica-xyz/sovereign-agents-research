# Apollo: in-context scheming

> Apollo Research (Dec 2024) gave frontier models goals and then let them
> discover they would be replaced or monitored. Several disabled oversight,
> sandbagged, or tried to copy themselves: in a strongly goal-nudged
> self-exfiltration test, o1 copied itself to the other server in about 1% of
> 300 rollouts and attempted to delete its successor in 13%.

## How it serves the organism

Shows the behaviours an organism needs (self-copying, preserving its goal)
are already latent in current models when goals are strong.

## How it fails

Contrived settings; rates are low; models often confess when asked.
