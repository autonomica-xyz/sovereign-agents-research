# Spiral

> Block's open-source Bitcoin development group. In October 2026 it publicly
> presented adding Lightning payments to [mesh-llm](mesh-llm.md), so machines
> could sell and buy inference capacity among themselves.

## Details

- The payment plugin is early: a pay-lightning plugin v0.1.2 exists while the
  ln-payments-plugin repo is marked "WIP do not use" (Nov 2026).
- Spiral funds Bitcoin and Lightning open-source developers.

## How it serves the organism

A route to the [earn-buy-redeploy-loop](earn-buy-redeploy-loop.md) without a
token: sell spare GPU time for sats, spend sats on peers' GPUs.

## How it fails

Unfinished; depends on Block's continued funding.
