# ERC-4337

> Account abstraction without a hard fork: users send "UserOperations" to
> bundlers, which submit them through a singleton EntryPoint contract;
> paymasters can sponsor gas. The EntryPoint went live in March 2023. It lets
> a wallet be code with any rule: multisig, spending limits, session keys,
> social recovery, passkeys.

## How it serves the organism

Lets its wallet encode limits and recovery in a contract, and pay gas in
tokens or have it sponsored.

## How it fails

Bundlers and paymasters can refuse service; the rules are public on-chain.
