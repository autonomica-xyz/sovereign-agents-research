# Azure confidential computing

> Microsoft offers confidential VMs on AMD SEV-SNP and Intel TDX, SGX
> enclaves, and confidential GPU VMs with NVIDIA H100, plus the Azure
> Attestation service.

## How it serves the organism

Large-scale confidential GPUs on demand.

## How it fails

Account KYC, billing and Microsoft's attestation service in the loop.
