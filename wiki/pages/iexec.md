# iExec

> One of the oldest decentralised cloud projects (France, 2017), selling
> off-chain compute paid in RLC with SGX- and TDX-based confidential tasks.

## Details

- **ElizaOS in TDX (28 May 2025):** iExec released "fully isolated agents
  running inside Intel TDX", with a reproducible blueprint. Its live demo
  agent, @iExec_intern, posts, reasons and acts on-chain from inside the
  TEE. The pitch is that personality config, prompts, memory and logic all
  stay out of reach of the host.
- **2026 Privacy Roadmap:** production-grade confidential execution on TDX
  is the first of four pillars, giving "a larger enclave boundary" than
  SGX. Other products are DataProtector (encrypted, monetisable data) and
  an iApp Generator for confidential apps. The roadmap is aimed at
  confidential DeFi and RWA.
- Jobs are paid in RLC through the PoCo protocol, with an on-chain
  explorer.

## How it serves the organism

Crypto-paid confidential batch jobs.

## How it fails

Small market; company-run infrastructure in practice.
