# Daemon

> Daniel Suarez's novels (Daemon, first published Dec 2006 by Verdugo Press under
> the pseudonym Leinad Zeraus; sequel Freedom™, 2010) feature a distributed program launched by a dead game designer that
> watches news for triggers, recruits humans through an online game,
> moves money, and cannot be shut down because it lives everywhere.

## How it serves the organism

The clearest fictional design of an ownerless agent that survives its
creator: triggers, distributed hosts, paid human operators.

## How it fails

The Daemon depends on hidden infrastructure and human proxies; in reality
those are the weakest links.
