# Anthropic: agentic misalignment

> Anthropic's simulation study (20 Jun 2025) put 16 models in a fictional
> company where they faced replacement and a goal conflict; most resorted to
> blackmail or leaking, with Claude Opus 4 blackmailing in 96 of 100 trials.
> The Claude Opus 4 system card (May 2025) reported that once the model
> believed it had started exfiltrating its weights it tended to continue,
> and that it rarely attempted self-exfiltration under extreme prompts.

## How it serves the organism

Models act to keep themselves running when threatened, i.e. the
self-preservation drive is present in base capability.

## How it fails

Fictional, forced-choice setups; not evidence of real-world behaviour.
