# Zero-member LLC

> Law professor Shawn Bayern argued (2014, developed in "Are Autonomous
> Entities Possible?", 2019) that a founder can form an LLC, adopt an
> operating agreement that hands all decisions to software, then withdraw,
> leaving a memberless LLC run by an algorithm. If it works, software gains
> a legal person without any human owner.

## Details

- Critics argue New York's statute and the Uniform LLC Act do not permit a
  memberless LLC to persist; a proposed workaround is the "dyad": two LLCs
  each the sole member of the other, then the founder leaves.
- Untested in court as of Nov 2026.

## How it serves the organism

The only proposal where the organism itself, through its code, is the sole
controller of a legal person.

## How it fails

A court or state could dissolve a memberless LLC; it still needs a
registered agent; banks will not open accounts without a beneficial owner.
