> 2024) that became one of the most-followed AI accounts. It earns from
> token on Virtuals; Spirit Index (2026) records a peak market cap near $700M
> later corrected to about $150M, subscription revenue "planned", and a token
> buyback mechanism. Revenue figures are not published.

## How it serves the organism

Shows attention converting into a token treasury; subscription income is still a plan, not a measured number.

## How it fails

Owned and operated by its team; X account and token are its only bodies.
