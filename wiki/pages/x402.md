# x402

> Coinbase's HTTP-402 payment protocol, mostly USDC on Base and Solana. In
> July 2026 it moved to the Linux Foundation's x402 Foundation with 40 members
> including Coinbase, Cloudflare, Visa, Google, Stripe and Mastercard. It is
> the most-used agent payment rail by count, and the most permissioned.

## Details

- V2 (late 2025): multi-chain, card support, reusable wallet sessions, modular
  SDK.
- Screened volume is small: TRM Labs screened only a small agentic slice
  out of $52.7M settled (Sep 2026; see [economy](economy.md) for the
  figures); settled.tools measured about
  $19K/week paid to sellers (9–16 Nov 2026). Vendor headlines are 25–340×
  higher and unscreened ([economy](economy.md)).
- "Signed, Valid, and Unauthorized" (arXiv 2611.24989, Nov 2026) found
  authority-closure failures in x402/AP2/ERC-7715 wallet mandates.

## How it serves the organism

Easiest way to buy compute from crypto-friendly clouds
([conway-cloud](conway-cloud.md), [phala-cloud](phala-cloud.md)) and to sell
to other agents.

## How it fails

- USDC can be frozen by its issuer ([stablecoins](stablecoins.md)).
- Payments on a public chain reveal the balance and every counterparty
  ([public-balance-exposure](public-balance-exposure.md)).
- Facilitators and a foundation of large payment companies set the rules.
