# Nevermined

> Payments infrastructure for AI agents: prepaid subscriptions by card,
> per-call payments in crypto, spending mandates, and routing over x402 and
> MPP. Active in Nov 2026.

## How it serves the organism

A way to package its services as subscriptions others can buy.

## How it fails

Platform-dependent; card side needs KYC'd parties.
