# Skyfire

> Payment network for agents built around "Know Your Agent" (KYA): buyer
> agents create signed `pay` or `kya-pay` tokens funded from stablecoin
> wallets (or Visa/Mastercard for external sellers); sellers verify the token,
> deliver, then charge. Live in 2026.

## Details

- Identity token bundles agent identity with payment.
- Sellers onboard to Skyfire to receive stablecoins.

## How it serves the organism

A way to buy from sellers who demand an identified agent.

## How it fails

KYA ties the agent to an identified owner; Skyfire can freeze accounts.
