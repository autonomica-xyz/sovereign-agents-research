# b-money

> Wei Dai's 1998 proposal for "a scheme for a group of untraceable digital
> pseudonyms to pay each other with money and to enforce contracts amongst
> themselves without outside help". Money created by solving computational
> problems; contracts enforced by deposits and arbitrators. Cited in the
> Bitcoin whitepaper.

## Details

- Motivated directly by Tim May's crypto-anarchy: a community where
  "the threat of violence is impotent" because participants cannot be
  linked to real names.
- Two protocols: every participant keeps the ledger (impractical), or a
  subset of servers with deposits keeps it (a precursor of staking).

## How it serves the organism

The first design for economic actors that are only keys, which is what an
organism is.

## How it fails

Never implemented; enforcement relied on reputational deposits.
