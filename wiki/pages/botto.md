# Botto

> A "decentralised autonomous artist" since 2021: a model generates work,
> a DAO votes on which piece to mint, and weekly auctions sell it. Cumulative
> sales exceed $6M; 2025 alone brought 644.7 ETH (about $1.84M) before fees.
> The longest-running AI entity that earns from selling a product, not a token.

## Details

- 85% of auction proceeds go to Botto (rewards, treasury); weekly SuperRare
  auctions continue in 2026; shown at Art Basel Hong Kong (Mar 2026).

## How it serves the organism

Evidence that an AI entity can earn real revenue from goods for years
([op-non-token-revenue](op-non-token-revenue.md)).

## How it fails

Governed by a DAO of humans who choose what is sold and run the
infrastructure; not self-custodial in the organism sense.
