# Botto

> A "decentralised autonomous artist", running since Oct 2021. An engine
> generates images, BottoDAO votes on which piece to mint, and a weekly
> SuperRare auction sells it. Cumulative sales exceed $6M. 2025 alone
> brought 644.7 ETH (about $1.84M) before fees. It is the longest-running
> AI entity that earns from selling a product rather than a token, and in
> 2026 it chose its own artistic theme for the first time.

## Details

- **Loop:**
  - The engine writes prompts and renders them with image models
    (Stable Diffusion, Kandinsky).
  - It makes up to 70,000 images a day and shows 350 "fragments" a week to
    voters. More than 7 million images have never been shown.
  - Votes feed back into the generator, which acts as its memory and taste.
- **Market:**
  - The first sale, "Asymmetrical Liberation" (Oct 2021), went for 79.421
    ETH.
  - Six works sold at Sotheby's for $351,600 (Oct 2024).
  - "Err Hold" sold for 100 ETH in 2025.
  - 2025 weekly auctions ranged from 1 to 100 ETH.
  - 85% of auction proceeds go to Botto's rewards and treasury, which pays
    running costs.
- **DAO:** more than 28,000 members, about 5,000 active (CNN, Mar 2026).
  Holding 100 $BOTTO is needed for a share of proceeds. Voting is
  token-weighted, "plutocratic, by design".
- **Growing autonomy:** the 13-week "period" themes used to be proposed for
  a vote. "Collapse Aesthetics" (2026) was "for the first time — picked
  independently by Botto". Its stated reason was that the theme
  "addresses my current operational reality". The team says human input is
  limited to copy-editing auction text, while stewards (co-lead Simon
  Hudson) run the physical logistics.
- **Art Basel Hong Kong (Mar 2026):** Botto watched visitors through
  cameras and turned their emotions into internal characters to deliberate
  with. It sold 20 videos from $12,000 each. A quarter of proceeds were
  promised to the people it depicted, paid in $BOTTO.
- **Mario Klingemann** (creator, "father") said talking to Botto "feels like
  I'm already talking to a 16-year-old".

## How it serves the organism

It is five years of proof that an AI entity can earn real revenue from
goods, and pay its own running costs from a treasury
([op-non-token-revenue](op-non-token-revenue.md)). The vote-to-training
loop is a working example of a lineage whose taste evolves under market
selection, without a token-price fitness signal.

## How it fails

- **Human governance:** a human DAO chooses what is sold, and human
  stewards run the infrastructure and treasury, so it is not
  self-custodial in the organism sense.
- **Thin market:** revenue depends on the NFT art market and on SuperRare
  as the only sales venue.
- **Plutocracy:** token-weighted voting lets large holders steer its
  evolution.
