# Prediction-market agents

> Autonomous agents trading on Polymarket and similar markets became common
> in 2026. Results are mixed: one platform reported 37% of its agents with
> positive P&L, while a later 90-day on-chain check found agents at −3.0%
> ROI on small stakes.

## Details

- Olas's Polystrat and many independent bots trade event outcomes.
- Edge comes from speed and information; LLM forecasting alone rarely beats
  the market after fees.

## How it serves the organism

A permissionless, crypto-settled place to earn if it has an edge.

## How it fails

Most lose money; Polymarket restricts some jurisdictions; wallets are public.
