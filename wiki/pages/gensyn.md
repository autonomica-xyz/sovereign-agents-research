# Gensyn

> A network for verified machine-learning work. Mainnet launched 22 Apr 2026
> with Delphi (prediction/evidence markets) as the first app and the $AI token
> on 29 Apr. Its RL Swarm training program is paused.

## Details

- Verde: verification of ML execution (arXiv 2502.19405), in production.
- Delphi: $310,004 lifetime volume, $5,143 protocol revenue (22 Nov 2026,
  dashboard).

## How it serves the organism

A place to earn by doing verifiable ML work, and a proof system for
outsourced compute it buys.

## How it fails

Demand is small; one network's governance and token economics set the terms.
