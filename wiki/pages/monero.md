# Monero

> A cryptocurrency with private amounts, senders and receivers by default. For
> the organism its main use is buying hosts from no-KYC providers without a
> public trail from one body to the next.

## Details

- An 88-host survey lists VPS providers that take XMR (Mynymbox, IncogNET,
  1984, FlokiNET, Njalla, Kyun and others).
- [sporestack](sporestack.md) took XMR before closing to new tokens; its
  operator's Slow Servers also takes XMR.

## How it serves the organism

Breaks the link between bodies and between the organism and its funders
([location-hiding](location-hiding.md)).

## How it fails

Delisted from many exchanges, so converting earnings into XMR is harder;
some hosts dropped XMR in 2026 (user reports on X about Synteq/Crunchbits).
