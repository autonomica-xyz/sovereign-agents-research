# Vitalik Buterin

> Ethereum co-founder (@VitalikButerin, ~7.9M followers, Nov 2026). In 2014
> he named the DAO/DAC/DA taxonomy, and in 2026 he became the loudest critic
> of "sovereign AI" hype. His argument: an agent that runs on OpenAI or
> Anthropic models "is not even 'self-sovereign'". At the same time he
> published the most concrete self-sovereign inference stack: local-first
> LLMs, sandboxing, Tor, and anonymous payment through zkAPI.

## Details

- **2014:** "DAOs, DACs, DAs and More" gave the vocabulary for autonomous
  agents on a chain. See [daos-dacs-das](daos-dacs-das.md).
- **22 Jan 2026:** "2026 is the year we take back lost ground in computing
  self-sovereignty."
- **19 Feb 2026, reply to Sigil Wen's Automaton launch:** "Bro, this is
  wrong. Lengthening the feedback distance between humans and AIs is not a
  good thing for the world … the models are run by openai and anthropic, so
  the thing is not even 'self-sovereign'". He also wrote: "The point of
  ethereum is to set *us* free, not to create something else that goes off
  and does some stuff freely". The reply drew 3.5K likes and 570K
  impressions. See [conway-automaton](conway-automaton.md).
- **2 Apr 2026, "My self-sovereign / local / private / secure LLM setup":**
  - The goals: all inference local-first, all files local, sandbox
    everything.
  - He quotes security reports on OpenClaw, including one that "Roughly 15%
    of the skills we've seen contained malicious instructions".
  - He warns readers not to copy the setup as if it were secure.
- **28 May 2026:** 96 GB is a practical "cap" on local RAM for models.
- **1 Jun 2026:** after Meta's support agent let attackers take over
  Instagram accounts, he called for "self-sovereign identity, data and
  money" and "CROPS AI".
- **16 Oct 2026, "paranoid mode":** [zkAPI](zkapi.md) plus a Tor circuit
  per conversation, cover traffic, local prompt rewriting, and a bwrap
  sandbox for his pi harness.

## How it serves the organism

His critique states the organism's first law from the outside: a body that
rents its mind from a lab is the lab's. His own stack is an inventory of
privacy organs, built for a human principal but usable by an agent that is
its own principal:

- local or anonymised inference;
- unlinkable payment;
- network anonymity;
- sandboxed tools.

## How it fails

(As a source) He opposes autonomous AI as an end in itself and argues for
short human feedback loops. His tooling is personal and experimental. zkAPI
rests on a single-party trusted setup.
