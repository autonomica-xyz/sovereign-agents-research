# Penumbra

> Cosmos chain (live since Jul 2024) where every asset sits in one shielded
> pool, with a private batch DEX and private staking. Penumbra Labs
> announced it is winding down; the chain was still producing blocks on
> 23 Nov 2026 with community validators.

## How it serves the organism

Private swaps between IBC assets.

## How it fails

Its main developer left; a chain without a funded team is drifting
toward dormancy.
