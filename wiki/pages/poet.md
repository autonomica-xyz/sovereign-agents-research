# POET

> Wang, Lehman, Clune and Stanley (Uber AI, 2019): co-evolve environments and
> agents, transferring agents between environments, so solutions emerge that
> direct optimisation cannot find.

## Details

- Agents solve hard terrain only via stepping stones from other environments.

## How it serves the organism

Argues for keeping a population across varied markets and hosts, transferring
successful variants between niches.

## How it fails

Simulation only.
