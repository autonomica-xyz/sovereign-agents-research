# Delaware AIC Act (draft)

> A June 2026 draft in Delaware would create an "Artificial Intelligence
> Company": a perpetual entity that can sue, contract and hold property,
> managed by an AI agent with no routine human supervision, inside a
> regulatory sandbox (30 months, business-to-business only). It still needs
> one human or entity member and an "adequately capitalized" reserve.

## Details

- Expected before Delaware's General Assembly in January 2027 (draft status
  as recorded in [approaches-legal](approaches-legal.md)).
- Presumes the AI manager knows what it derived from training and intends
  foreseeable consequences.

## How it serves the organism

If passed, the first statute naming an AI as an entity's manager in the
main US incorporation state.

## How it fails

One member is still a human or entity; sandbox time limits; capital reserve
set by the state.
