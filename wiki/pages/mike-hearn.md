# Mike Hearn

> Early Bitcoin developer (bitcoinj) who in 2013 publicly described
> self-sustaining Bitcoin "agents" that rent servers, sell services and
> reproduce, at the Turing Festival in Edinburgh. He left Bitcoin in January
> 2016, declaring the experiment had failed.

## Details

- His talk popularised the Bitcoin Wiki "Agents" design
  ([bitcoin-wiki-agents](bitcoin-wiki-agents.md)), including self-driving cars
  that own themselves and pay for their own repairs.
- Later worked on Corda (R3) and Conveyor; not active in agent work.

## How it serves the organism

Gave the organism idea its first public pitch to a general audience.

## How it fails

(As a source) A talk, not code; none of his agents were built.
