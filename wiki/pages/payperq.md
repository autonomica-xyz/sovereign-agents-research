# PayPerQ

> A pay-per-query AI service: top up from 10 cents with crypto (including
> Lightning) or card, no subscription and no email signup required.
> An inference-only rail.

## Details

- Inference access only; no hosting.

## How it serves the organism

A fallback brain when its own hosts are down.

## How it fails

One company that sees prompts unless encrypted; a single throat for this rail.
