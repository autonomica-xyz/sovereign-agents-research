# ASAL

> Sakana AI and MIT (Dec 2024; Artificial Life journal 2025): use
> vision-language foundation models to search simulation parameters for
> target behaviours, open-ended novelty and diverse lifeforms. Found new
> Lenia and Boids lifeforms and open-ended cellular automata.

## Details

- Substrates: Boids, Particle Life, Game of Life, Lenia, neural cellular
  automata.
- Three modes: supervised target, open-endedness, illumination (diversity).

## How it serves the organism

A way to measure "interestingness" and novelty with foundation models, which a
lineage could use to choose which children to fund.

## How it fails

Pure simulation; no resources, keys or adversaries.
