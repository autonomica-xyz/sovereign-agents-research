# Briar

> Peer-to-peer messenger that syncs over Tor, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth with no
> servers; designed for activists and internet shutdowns.

## Details

- Contacts are added by key exchange; messages stored only on devices.

## How it serves the organism

A design reference for messaging that keeps working when infrastructure
is shut down.

## How it fails

Built for humans on phones; not a programmable agent channel.
