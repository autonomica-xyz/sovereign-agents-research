# Tribler

> TU Delft research lab and the long-running Tribler peer-to-peer project
> (anonymous BitTorrent over its own onion routing, IPv8 networking). In
> 2026 it published the superorganism experiment: seedbox nodes that buy
> their own VPSes with Bitcoin, spawn children and die when broke.

## Details

- Experiment repo: github.com/Tribler/superorganism-experiment
  ([eternalseedbox](eternalseedbox.md), [superorganism](superorganism.md)).
- Paper arXiv 2609.05431 (simulation: 268 births, 190 deaths over 510 days).
- Depended on [sporestack](sporestack.md), which closed to new signups on
  7 Nov 2026, and on one GitHub repo for code
  ([single-code-source](single-code-source.md)).

## How it serves the organism

The only academic group running a real (if simulated-income) earn-spawn-die
loop with heredity.

## How it fails

(For its organisms) Parents know children's keys; secrets sit in plaintext on
rented VPSes; one supplier and one code source.
