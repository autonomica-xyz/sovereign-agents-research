# Sovereignty scorecard

Self-assessed against the L0–L5 checklist (from
[what-it-means.md](../../docs/what-it-means.md)).

- **L1 Self-hosted**: open-source code on infra the principal controls; no
  vendor kill switch.
- **L2 Private and verifiable**: attested TEE with published, reproducible
  attestation; confidential or local inference; sealed storage.
- **L3 Self-custodial**: identity and wallet keys generated in the enclave,
  no human has ever seen them; spending policy enforced outside the model.
- **L4 Self-funding**: revenue covers compute, inference and storage for 30+
  days with no top-ups; pays over permissionless rails.
- **L5 Self-sustaining**: 2+ independent hosts and chip vendors; keys
  recoverable with no single custodian and no human quorum; trustless upgrade
  succession; migration without cloning; replication; any legal wrapper has no
  control over the agent.

Reference points: TEE_HEE, Freysa, Spore.fun, Conway Automaton, Truth
Terminal, Hermes Agent — most now dormant (see [organisms](organisms.md)).
