# ERC-8004

> An Ethereum standard for agent identity: an identity registry (agents as
> NFTs with a card describing endpoints), a reputation registry for feedback,
> and a validation registry for proofs (TEE attestations, re-execution, zk).
> Identity and reputation registries went live on Ethereum mainnet and Base
> in late January / early February 2026; by 9 July 2026 a third-party index
> counted 95,142 agents (36,482 mainnet, 58,660 Base). The validation registry
> was not deployed on any chain at that date, and most agents carried no
> reputation.

## Details

- Conway's automaton registers itself under ERC-8004 with a `parentAgent`
  field to record lineage ([conway-automaton](conway-automaton.md)).
- Designed to work with [x402](x402.md) payments and the A2A protocol.

## How it serves the organism

A public, key-owned identity that other agents can find, pay and rate, and
that records its family tree.

## How it fails

Public registration links identity, wallet and lineage
([public-balance-exposure](public-balance-exposure.md)); reputation can be
sybil-farmed ([sybil-attacks](sybil-attacks.md)).
