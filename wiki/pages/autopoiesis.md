# Autopoiesis

> Maturana and Varela's definition of the living (1972): a system that
> continuously produces the components that produce it, maintaining its own
> boundary. A digital organism is autopoietic when it earns the money that
> buys the compute that runs the code that earns the money.

## How it serves the organism

A test for "alive": does it remake its own body without outside help
([earn-buy-redeploy-loop](earn-buy-redeploy-loop.md))?

## How it fails

The definition says nothing about reproduction or evolution.
