# terra0

> Berlin art and research project (Paul Seidler, Paul Kolling, Max Hampshire)
> whose 2016 whitepaper proposed a forest that owns itself: an Ethereum
> contract sells logging licences, repays its initial investors, then holds
> its own land. The full system was never deployed; later work used NFTs,
> small experiments and, by 2025, a German non-profit plus a DAO.

## Details

- Oracles (satellite imagery) tell the contract how much wood exists, a
  physical-world sensor for an economic agent.
- The legal gap (a forest cannot hold title) forced a human wrapper, the
  same gap an AI organism faces ([legal-personhood](legal-personhood.md)).
- Code: github.com/terra0project/source.

## How it serves the organism

Template for a non-human owner that earns by selling a resource it holds,
and for using a legal wrapper as a shell.

## How it fails

Land and timber are under state law; the self-ownership remained an artwork.
