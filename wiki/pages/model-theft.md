# Model and state theft

> Weights leak: Meta's LLaMA weights spread from a torrent within a week of
> limited release (Mar 2023), and Mistral's CEO confirmed in Jan 2024 that
> "miqu-1-70b" was an older Mistral model leaked by an employee of an
> early-access customer. For an organism, the valuable secrets are its keys,
> memory and fine-tunes, and host compromise steals them
> ([phala-inference-breach-2026-11](phala-inference-breach-2026-11.md)).

## How it serves the organism

If its mind is open weights, theft of weights costs nothing; protect keys
and memory instead.

## How it fails

A copied organism with the same keys is a twin that can spend its money
([op-anti-twin](op-anti-twin.md)).
