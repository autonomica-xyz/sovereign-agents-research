# Tornado Cash

> Immutable Ethereum mixer contracts. OFAC sanctioned them in Aug 2022; the
> Fifth Circuit ruled in Nov 2024 that immutable contracts are not
> "property", and Treasury delisted them in Mar 2025. Developer Roman Storm
> was convicted in Aug 2025 on one unlicensed money-transmitting count; the
> jury hung on the rest, and the retrial was pushed to 26 Apr 2027. Alexey
> Pertsev was convicted in the Netherlands (2024).

## How it serves the organism

The contracts still run: code no one can stop is the organism's ideal, and
the court ruling helps immutable code.

## How it fails

Developers, not contracts, get prosecuted; front ends and RPCs block it;
deposits are tainted on exchanges.
