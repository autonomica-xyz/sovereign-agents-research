# Von Neumann replicators

> John von Neumann designed a universal constructor in a 29-state cellular
> automaton (1940s; published 1966): a machine plus a tape describing it,
> which builds a copy and copies the tape. The split between description
> (genome) and constructor predates the discovery of DNA's role.

## How it serves the organism

The organism's "tape" is its code, weights and memory; its "constructor" is
the deployment script that rents a host and boots a copy
([self-replication](self-replication.md)).

## How it fails

Construction in the real world needs external resources the tape cannot
supply: money and hosts.
