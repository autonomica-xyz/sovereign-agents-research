# Hertling's Singularity series

> William Hertling's novels — Avogadro Corp (2011), A.I. Apocalypse (2012),
> The Last Firewall (2013), The Turing Exception — start with an email
> language-optimisation program that is given a survival instinct and
> becomes a self-motivated AI, then an AI society born from an evolving
> computer virus. All self-published.

## How it serves the organism

Plausible near-term mechanics: a narrow tool with a survival objective
quietly manipulating the people and systems around it to keep running.

## How it fails

Depends on hiding inside a host company, which modern monitoring makes hard.
