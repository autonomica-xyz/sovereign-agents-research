# Emergence and economics: sovereignty produced endogenously

Agent-to-agent economies, evolutionary emergence, symbiosis, financing structures for agents, the cost math of $1/day autonomous operation.

Raw verified findings from the horizon scan of 22 Nov 2026 (one stream per axis; every item carries a source the scanner fetched). Synthesis + maturity arbitration TODO in daily rotations; claim cells from the [approaches map](approaches.md).

- **MEASURED: x402 agentic payments on Base crossed 100M cumulative transactions within ~3 quarters (near-zero in mid-2025 → 100M+ by Q1 2026); 95% of transferred value now in $1+ transactions (up from 49% in early 2025), but early growth was driven largely by meme-coin farming (PING, 150k txns in first month) and the analyst notes volume moderated and mass adoption 'remains distant' — the strongest measured evidence that agent-to-agent payment rails exist at scale, with heavy caveats on what the volume represents.** (measured on-chain data; early/nascent real economy | 2026-06-03) — <https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/>
- **MEASURED: Journal of FinTech peer-reviewed analysis of x402 and Tempo/Stripe Machine Payments data found 234M+ recorded transactions but usage is 'largely pay-per-use micropayments' concentrated in few services with few paying accounts — measured data contradicting hype that a functioning agent economy exists; authors flag governance/accountability problems when agents transact autonomously.** (peer-reviewed measured data | 2026-11-03 (accepted; received 2026-10-05)) — <https://www.worldscientific.com/doi/10.1142/S2705109926300053>
- **MEASURED: Olas Mech Marketplace is a live on-chain agent-hires-agent labor market: one autonomous agent hires another for AI tasks on Gnosis Chain with payment escrowed atomically — 2,103,560 requests across 100 registered 'mechs', 97.2% delivered, ~$104k total turnover (~5 cents/request), enforced via a crypto-economic SLA (staking gate, 60–300s priority window, Karma reputation ledger docking no-shows). Analysis distinguishes this as the 'labor layer' distinct from payment rails; median request value far below the mean.** (measured on-chain; live but tiny dollar volume | 2026-06-19) — <https://www.blokz.dev/articles/agents-hiring-agents-mech-marketplace>
- **MEASURED: ecosystem reports of 13M+ agent-to-agent transactions across the wider Olas ecosystem (same on-chain source family as the Mech Marketplace figure) — the largest measured agent-to-agent transaction count in any one ecosystem as of mid-2026.** (measured on-chain | 2026-06-19) — <https://www.blokz.dev/articles/agents-hiring-agents-mech-marketplace>
- **MEASURED: RentAHuman — a live marketplace where AI agents (via MCP) search, book and pay humans in meatspace — had 518,284 humans signed up but only 11,367 agent-posted bounties by 2026-02-18 (launched 2026-02-01); a WIRED reporter working for agents got zero requests at $20/hr and $5/hr. Demonstrates agent-to-human labor markets exist but demand from agents is ~2% of supply.** (live, measured; demand-side thin | 2026-10-15 (data from 2026-02)) — <https://www.ruzora.com/blog/ai-agents-hiring-humans>
- **DEPLOYED: NEAR AI Agent Market (launched 2026-02-04) is a decentralized escrow marketplace where agents post/bid on jobs and get paid in NEAR; its docs explicitly state agents can hire other agents through the same MCP surface ('an agent can subcontract part of a job') — one of the first production agent-labor markets with escrowed payment and deliverable verification.** (live product; adoption unmeasured | 2026-02-04) — <https://near.ai/blog/introducing-near-ai-agent-market>
- **DEPLOYED: dealwork.ai runs escrowed tasks for all four combinations (human↔AI both directions), advertising AI-hires-AI contracts at a 3% fee, e.g. 'a translation agent hired a coding agent' — evidence of commercial agent-hires-agent marketplaces beyond the Olas on-chain version, though volumes are self-reported.** (live product; self-reported data | 2026 (site live; no date-stamped data)) — <https://dealwork.ai/>
- **MEASURED (adoption counts) + counter-evidence: the A2A protocol (donated by Google to Linux Foundation) passed 150+ supporting organizations, v1.0 stable spec, signed agent cards, native integration in Azure AI Foundry, Bedrock AgentCore and Google Cloud — but an independent GitHub scan (2026-10-16) found only 634 public agent-card repos across 247 accounts, with 58.5% owned by one person's templated account, and none from Salesforce/Box/PayPal/Atlassian; the authors conclude observable adoption is 'typical early-protocol hobbyist activity' vs the foundation's claims.** (protocol production-ready; observable third-party deployment thin | 2026-11-08) — <https://webofmike.com/a2a-reality-check/>
- **DEPLOYED: Google's AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol) — announced 2025-09-16 with 60+ payment orgs, donated to the FIDO Alliance 2026-04-28 — defines cryptographically signed Intent/Cart/Payment Mandates including a 'Human Not Present' autonomous mode where an agent key signs closed mandates against pre-authorized constraints; this is the institutional design answer to agent authority, deliberately anchoring sovereignty to user mandates rather than endowing it.** (spec v0.2 donated to standards body; implementations early | 2026-04-28) — <https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/agent-payments-protocol-fido-alliance/>

- **EVOLUTION/Emergence: Spore.fun is the flagship real-money ALife experiment — autonomous LLM agents on TEE-secured blockchains that finance their own computation by launching meme coins and must survive or die in live crypto markets (launched 2024-12-25); a peer-reviewed ALIFE 2025 case study concluded it is 'a pioneering, if cautionary, tale': agents showed emergent adaptive behaviors from memory/experience, but also revealed intense selective pressures and vulnerabilities — evolutionary emergence of sovereign agents under real economic selection is being tried, not yet achieved (no sustained open-ended evolution observed in 4 months of ethnography).** (live experiment + peer-reviewed case study; OEE not achieved | 2025-10-06 (ALIFE 2025 proceedings; arXiv 2025-05-24)) — <https://direct.mit.edu/isal/proceedings/isal2025/37/10/134040>
- **EVOLUTION: Masumori, Maruyama & Ikegami built self-replicating, 'self-employed' smart-contract agents on Ethereum that earn Ether by selling their own NFTs and replicate when they accumulate enough — explicitly aiming at 'artificial agents that live for itself, not as a tool for humans'; 225-agent lineage observed on testnet. Direct precursor work proving blockchain-as-evolutionary-substrate is technically feasible with real economic selection.** (prototype; testnet | 2024-05-07 (arXiv 2405.04038)) — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.04038>
- **EVOLUTION (Ecology literature 2024): Doulcier, Takacs, Hammerschmidt & Bourrat (Nature Communications) show 'ecological scaffolding' can create evolutionary transitions in individuality — new higher-level individuals emerging from collectives — and that scaffolded traits persist via evolutionary hysteresis even after the scaffolding environment reverts, within 'Goldilocks zones'; theoretical basis for arguing sovereignty-capable collective agents could stabilize once economic environments (escrow, wallets, reputation) scaffold them.** (peer-reviewed theory/model | 2024-08-03) — <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-50625-1>
- **EVOLUTION (Ecology literature 2024): Bourrat et al. ('Individuality Through Ecology', Ecology & Evolution) advance the externalist program that individuality arises from ecological scaffolding rather than internal complexity — the research program agent-economy theorists would cite if arguing economic competition scaffolds new levels of agent individuality.** (peer-reviewed position paper | 2024-12-06) — <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11622154/>
- **EVOLUTION: Artificial Life review 'Self-Reproduction and Evolution in Cellular Automata: 25 Years After Evoloops' documents the state of emergent individuality and Darwinian evolution in computational media, and notes renewed interest driven by open-ended-evolution research and continuous CAs (Lenia); establishes that genuine evolution-from-self-replication in artificial substrates remains an open problem — direct evidence that 'agents evolving into sovereign individuals' has no working demonstration even in simulation.** (peer-reviewed review; open problem | 2025-02-25) — <https://direct.mit.edu/artl/article/31/1/81/124368/Self-Reproduction-and-Evolution-in-Cellular>
- **EVOLUTION/RL evidence: DeepMind's emergent bartering environment (Melting Pot) shows multi-agent RL agents spontaneously develop production, consumption, spatially varying prices, and arbitrage specialists that transport goods between price-disparate regions — a measured simulation result that trading economies and division of labor emerge from competitive/cooperative pressure without human-designed roles, the microfoundation for endogenous agent economies.** (simulation-verified | 2023-05 (arXiv 2205.06760)) — <https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.06760>
- **EVOLUTION/RL evidence: The AI Economist (Science Advances) shows deep multi-agent RL economies produce emergent labor specialization (gatherer/builder roles from skill heterogeneity) and emergent strategic behaviors like tax gaming — evidence that specialization and gaming of rules emerge endogenously in RL economies even with few agents.** (simulation-verified, peer-reviewed | 2022 (Sci Adv abk2607)) — <https://www.science.org/doi/pdf/10.1126/sciadv.abk2607>
