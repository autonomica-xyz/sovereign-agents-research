# Oasis ROFL

> Runtime Off-chain Logic: apps run in Intel TEEs (SGX or TDX VMs), attest,
> register on Oasis Sapphire, and get keys from Oasis's decentralised key
> manager. Mainnet launched 2 Jul 2025, pitched for AI agents.

## How it serves the organism

Contract-governed key release and a marketplace of TEE hosts.

## How it fails

Intel-only; key manager is itself a TEE committee.
