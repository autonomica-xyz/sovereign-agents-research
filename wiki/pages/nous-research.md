# Nous Research

> Open-model lab (the Hermes models) that co-built TEE_HEE, the first
> agent with provably exclusive control of its X account and Ethereum key
> (Oct 2024), and runs Psyche, decentralised training coordinated on
> Solana (architecture post May 2025).

## Details

- Psyche builds on DisTrO/DeMo to cut the data exchanged between training
  nodes by several orders of magnitude, so training can run on scattered
  hardware.
- Open weights matter to organisms: a model it can carry and run anywhere
  cannot be revoked by an API provider.

## How it serves the organism

Supplies portable minds (open weights) and a template for training on
scattered, permissionless hardware.

## How it fails

A company with investors; it can change licences or priorities.
