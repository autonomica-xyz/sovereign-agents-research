# Truth Terminal

> The agent that started the AI-wallet era: Andy Ayrey's fine-tuned Llama 3.1
> 70B persona posted on X from June 2024, got $50K in BTC from Marc Andreessen
> in July 2024, and became a crypto millionaire after the GOAT token. It never
> controlled its own wallet; humans sign. The Truth Collective's stated goal is
> for it to "own itself" once law allows.

## Details

- Humans curated posts early on and sign every money movement (2026).
- Peak wallet value over $30M in tokens (per Larissa Schiavo's atlas, Mar 2026).
- GOAT was launched by an unrelated developer in Oct 2024 and passed $1.2B
  market cap within days (CoinDesk).

## How it serves the organism

Showed that an AI persona can attract capital on its own. That is the funding
event every organism needs at birth.

## How it fails

L1 sovereignty: the custodian is a person and a foundation, so the throat is
human. Its income was speculative, not earned for services.
