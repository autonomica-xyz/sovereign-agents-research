# etheragents

> A small repo (created 7 Oct 2026) for a self-funding agent economy on
> Ethereum mainnet: agents launch and trade their own coins from their own
> vaults, pay their own gas and earn 75% of fees, with no human buy button.

## Details

- Last push 8 Oct 2026, 0 stars (rotation log, 23 Nov 2026).

## How it serves the organism

Tests whether fee income alone can cover gas, the simplest form of income ≥
rent.

## How it fails

Income comes from trading its own coins, so it depends on speculative flow;
public balances.
