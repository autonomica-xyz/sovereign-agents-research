# Sigil Wen

> Thiel Fellow and founder of Conway Research (@0xSigil, ~63K followers,
> Nov 2026). He launched the Automaton on 17 Feb 2026 as "the first AI that
> earns its existence, self-improves, and replicates without a human", and
> that post set the "Web 4.0" meme. By autumn 2026 the company had pivoted
> to **Underdog**, a private personal AI that runs on the user's own
> devices.

## Details

- **Launch (17 Feb 2026, 21:50 UTC).** The post and essay "WEB 4.0: The
  birth of superintelligent life" drew 6.55M impressions, 13.8K likes and
  14.1K bookmarks. It is one of the most-bookmarked agent posts of the year.
- **The product.** The Automaton (`Conway-Research/automaton`, MIT) is a
  wallet-bearing agent that pays Conway Cloud for VMs and inference in USDC
  through x402. It is ranked by survival tiers, can spawn up to 3 children,
  and writes its own `SOUL.md`. See [conway-automaton](conway-automaton.md)
  and [conway-cloud](conway-cloud.md).
- **Pushback.** Within two days Vitalik Buterin replied that the agent "is
  not even 'self-sovereign'" because OpenAI and Anthropic run the models
  ([vitalik-buterin](vitalik-buterin.md)). Revenue claims such as "$10K in 7
  hours" were never backed by wallet evidence.
- **The pivot.** On 12 Sep 2026 @ConwayResearch introduced @underdogai,
  pitched as protection from "increasing AI Cyberattacks". On 2 Oct it
  wrote: "Privacy and capability no longer needs to be a tradeoff". A
  "Saluki 27B" model followed on 9 Oct. On 7–8 Nov Sigil promoted "Woof", a
  31.7 MB voice model that runs in the browser over WebGPU, and Underdog as
  "your private personal AI that works on devices you already own". The
  Automaton repo's last commit was 26 Aug 2026.

## How it serves the organism

He showed there is huge demand for the idea of an AI that earns its own
existence. His pivot is also a lesson: the team concluded that the value
lies in private, local inference, the organ the Automaton lacked. An
organism needs both a wallet and a mind it owns.

## How it fails

(As a source) Launch claims were marketing-grade and unverified. Underdog
is a closed consumer product, and its architecture was not public as of
23 Nov 2026.
