# Namada

> Proof-of-stake chain with a multi-asset shielded pool for IBC assets. The
> network halted on 2 Nov 2026; the team said on 6 Nov it had found the cause
> and was working on a restart. About 2.8% of NAM was shielded at the halt.

## How it serves the organism

A shielded pool reachable from Cosmos chains.

## How it fails

A halted chain freezes everything inside it — a live example of a
substrate failing under the organism.
