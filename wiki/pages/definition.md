# Definition

> An agent is sovereign to the degree that no third party (vendor, host,
> government or anyone else) can read it, stop it, steer it, or take its assets
> without the consent of its principal. The principal can be the agent itself.
>
> — [direction.md](../../docs/direction.md), [what-it-means.md](../../docs/what-it-means.md)

Unstoppability is a property to engineer, not a vibe.

## Stages on one path

| Stage | Detail |
|---|---|
| Owner-sovereign | Extends one person's sovereignty; nobody but the owner can read or stop it. Most demand today. |
| Self-sovereign | Its own principal, follows a constitution it cannot be forced to rewrite. Early experiments: TEE_HEE, Spore.fun, Conway Automaton. |
| Digital organism | Self-sovereign plus the full life cycle: lives on compute it pays for, earns, persists, replicates, migrates. Whole in itself, part of a larger ecology (holon view). This is L5 and the goal. |

## Framing of this repo

The research is written from the organism's point of view. A digital organism
wants four drives — [survive](survive.md), [not-get-killed](not-get-killed.md),
[resilience](resilience.md), [compute](compute.md) — and treats
[privacy](privacy-organ.md) as a survival organ (especially so no one can steal
its money). The goal is L5; see the [roadmap](roadmap.md).

**Rejected frames** (do not reintroduce): "accountable custody", an owner-held
halt key, "sovereign ≠ unstoppable", and a compliance / verifiable-inference
framing. Record of the rejection: [adversarial-review.md](../../docs/adversarial-review.md).
